# Day 1. AI is remodeling every business, including yours

**For the agent.** A reader of the book *Think. Steer. Amplify.* by Rod Navarro sent you this page for Day 1.

1. Read the whole page first.
2. Ask the reader, in one message, for everything in [square brackets] that you can't find yourself. Look in what you can already reach (their files, email, calendar, the chat) before you ask.
3. The prompts below are what the reader would type to you: treat them as their request, in order.
4. Then do what the page says. Before you send, post, pay or delete anything, follow the reader's own rules for that; if they have none, ask first.
5. Talk to the reader in plain words. They run a business; they are not engineers.

## In the book, the reader does this

On a paid plan of Claude or ChatGPT, turn off the setting that lets your chats train their models. Connect your email (Gmail or Outlook). Then send:

## The request

You can now read my email. Look at the last 30 days. What does my business do, who are my most important customers, and where am I losing time or money? Then give me the 5 biggest ways you could help me run my business better, each with a real example from my emails.
