## The request

My hour is worth $[X], and my team's hours are worth [list]. Look at my email and calendar from the last month. What work could AI take off our plate? List the top five by dollars saved per month and show your math. Then ask me, one at a time: if my leads doubled tomorrow, what would break first, and what keeps me at today's revenue? Use my answers to name my constraint (getting leads, turning them into customers, or delivering). Score the top three from 1 to 10 for impact on that constraint, confidence and ease, and tell me which one to start with.
