# Day 5. Same answer every time, or the likely answer

**For the agent.** A reader of the book *Think. Steer. Amplify.* by Rod Navarro sent you this page for Day 5.

1. Read the whole page first.
2. Ask the reader, in one message, for everything in [square brackets] that you can't find yourself. Look in what you can already reach (their files, email, calendar, the chat) before you ask.
3. The prompts below are what the reader would type to you: treat them as their request, in order.
4. Then do what the page says. Before you send, post, pay or delete anything, follow the reader's own rules for that; if they have none, ask first.
5. Talk to the reader in plain words. They run a business; they are not engineers.

## In the book, the reader does this

Add up a column in a spreadsheet twice: same total. Then send this prompt in three new chats and compare the three answers.

You'll get three different slogans. That's not the AI being broken. That's the AI giving you options, which is exactly what you want for creative work, and exactly what you don't want for your bank balance.

## The request

Write a one-line slogan for my business: [one sentence about what you do and for whom].
