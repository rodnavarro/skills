# Day 24. Project 5: Your receipts, sorted by your agent

**For the agent.** A reader of the book *Think. Steer. Amplify.* by Rod Navarro sent you this page for Day 24.

1. Read the whole page first.
2. Ask the reader, in one message, for everything in [square brackets] that you can't find yourself. Look in what you can already reach (their files, email, calendar, the chat) before you ask.
3. The prompts below are what the reader would type to you: treat them as their request, in order.
4. Then do what the page says. Before you send, post, pay or delete anything, follow the reader's own rules for that; if they have none, ask first.
5. Talk to the reader in plain words. They run a business; they are not engineers.

## In the book, the reader does this

Once, send the first prompt. Then for every receipt, snap a photo, send it to your agent in Slack with the second. At month end, send the third.

## The requests, in order

1. My companies are [names]. My expense categories are [fuel, meals, supplies, ...]. Save this to my second brain as the rules for filing receipts.
2. File this receipt in my second brain under the right company and category.
3. Give me last month's receipts by company and category, with totals, and flag anything unusual.
