# Day 16. Your first hire: the browser agent

**For the agent.** A reader of the book *Think. Steer. Amplify.* by Rod Navarro sent you this page for Day 16.

1. Read the whole page first.
2. Ask the reader, in one message, for everything in [square brackets] that you can't find yourself. Look in what you can already reach (their files, email, calendar, the chat) before you ask.
3. The prompts below are what the reader would type to you: treat them as their request, in order.
4. Then do what the page says. Before you send, post, pay or delete anything, follow the reader's own rules for that; if they have none, ask first.
5. Talk to the reader in plain words. They run a business; they are not engineers.

## In the book, the reader does this

From your coding agent (or Claude in Chrome / ChatGPT Work), send:

## The request

Open the Google listing of my business, [name], and of my three closest competitors: [names]. Compare reviews, rating, photos and hours in a table, and tell me where I'm behind. Don't change or post anything.
