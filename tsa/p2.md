# Day 2. AI is an instrument, and you are the musician

**For the agent.** A reader of the book *Think. Steer. Amplify.* by Rod Navarro sent you this page for Day 2.

1. Read the whole page first.
2. Ask the reader, in one message, for everything in [square brackets] that you can't find yourself. Look in what you can already reach (their files, email, calendar, the chat) before you ask.
3. The prompts below are what the reader would type to you: treat them as their request, in order.
4. Then do what the page says. Before you send, post, pay or delete anything, follow the reader's own rules for that; if they have none, ask first.
5. Talk to the reader in plain words. They run a business; they are not engineers.

## In the book, the reader does this

Paste the complaint and send the first prompt. Then send the second one and compare the two replies.

Read them side by side. The first reply is polite and could have come from any company. The second one could only have come from yours. Everything that changed between them came from you.

## The requests, in order

1. Write a reply to this customer complaint: [paste it].
2. Write it again. This customer has been with us for [years] and [what you know about them]. I want to keep them, and I can offer [what you can offer]. Write it the way I talk: [two words about your tone].
