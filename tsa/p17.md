# Day 17. Project 3: Get found on Google

**For the agent.** A reader of the book *Think. Steer. Amplify.* by Rod Navarro sent you this page for Day 17.

1. Read the whole page first.
2. Ask the reader, in one message, for everything in [square brackets] that you can't find yourself. Look in what you can already reach (their files, email, calendar, the chat) before you ask.
3. The prompts below are what the reader would type to you: treat them as their request, in order.
4. Then do what the page says. Before you send, post, pay or delete anything, follow the reader's own rules for that; if they have none, ask first.
5. Talk to the reader in plain words. They run a business; they are not engineers.

## In the book, the reader does this

From your coding agent, with your browser, send:

## The request

Using my browser, go to Google Business Profile and check whether [my business] in [my city] has a profile. If it doesn't, guide me through creating one step by step: you fill in the forms, I sign in, approve each step and do the verification. If it does, compare it with my three closest competitors and fix what's missing or weak: categories, services, hours, description, photos and a first post in my voice. Show me every change before you save it, and don't reply to any review without my OK.
