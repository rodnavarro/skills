# Day 28. Project 6: Answer every new lead in 5 minutes

**For the agent.** A reader of the book *Think. Steer. Amplify.* by Rod Navarro sent you this page for Day 28.

1. Read the whole page first.
2. Ask the reader, in one message, for everything in [square brackets] that you can't find yourself. Look in what you can already reach (their files, email, calendar, the chat) before you ask.
3. The prompts below are what the reader would type to you: treat them as their request, in order.
4. Then do what the page says. Before you send, post, pay or delete anything, follow the reader's own rules for that; if they have none, ask first.
5. Talk to the reader in plain words. They run a business; they are not engineers.

## The request

I want every new lead answered within 5 minutes, by an agent that keeps the conversation going until it books a visit or a call. My leads come from [Facebook lead forms / my website form / texts]. My lead rules are in [my second brain / this document]. Compare the routes that fit my setup (Meta's Business Agent, Zapier or Make with Twilio, a ready-made system, or my own always-on computer) with the monthly cost of each, and recommend one. Then set it up with me: the agent answers on its own under my rules, follows up if the lead goes quiet, stops on STOP, keeps follow-ups between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m., logs everything in my lead sheet, and hands me only the exceptions in my rules. Before turning it on, test it with my ten punch-list leads and show me every conversation.
